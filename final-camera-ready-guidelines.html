5th International Conference on Data, Decision and Systems 2026 (ICDDS 2026)

Guidelines for Camera Ready Submission

The authors are requested to read and understand the instructions of this document to prepare and submit the camera-ready copy of their paper. The authors are requested to complete all the camera ready submission before the deadline. Any non-compliance to the instruction may lead to forfeiture of your paper acceptance for further publication.

1. Preparation of Camera Ready Copy (CRC)

  1. Revise and modify your paper based on the reviewer's comments/suggestions.
  2. The reviewer's comments are available on the CMT portal of the conference
  3. The paper format strictly adheres to the IEEE conference template with correct font size, margins, double-column etc.
  4. The length of the manuscript should be as per conference guidelines.
  5. The CRC Copy should be identical in content to the original accepted version except the reviewer's requested revisions.
  6. The paper should have appropriate citation for all the reference material.
  7. The authors and their order should be same as submitted initially in the CMT portal. Please do not add or delete or change the order of authors in the paper. This will be checked strictly for any non-compliance.
  8. The camera ready submissions will be again sent for plagiarism check. The final paper should have acceptable similarity threshold as per the conference guidelines.
  9. The camera ready paper should be submitted in PDF format (size less than 5 MB).

2. PDF eXpress Check

  1. IEEE PDF eXpress tool must be used to generate the PDF format of all papers.
  2. PDF eXpress site (https://ieee-pdf-express.org/account/login) which is IEEE's online file conversion/PDF validation tool, will assist authors in preparing suitable electronic files of their final papers. PDF eXpress tool helps authors convert their papers into IEEE Xplore-compatible PDF files (conversion function) or to check their own PDF files for IEEE Xplore compatibility (PDF validation function).
  3. To generate IEEE Xplore compatible PDF file of your paper, follow given below steps
    • Create your IEEE PDF eXpress account: https://ieee-pdf-express.org/account/login
    • Conference ID: 72315X
    • The first time you access the system, please follow the link to the new user. Once you have registered as a new user:
      • Upload the source file (containing your paper) for conversion
      • Receive by e-mail the IEEE Xplore-compatible PDF of your paper.
  4. When you have checked and satisfied with the PDF file, then click on the "approve" link on the PDF express site.
  5. The certified acceptable file you receive from PDF eXpress might have a filename in the form PID123.pdf. You should rename this exact file to the following naming convention: ICDDS26_XXX.pdf, where XXX is the ID assigned to the paper on CMT Platform.

3. Submission of Camera Ready Paper

  1. Upload the renamed PDF express generated PDF file of your paper (ICDDS26_XXX.pdf, XXX is the CMT paper id) on the CMT portal of the conference.
  2. The submission should be completed before 30 Oct 2026 as given on the conference website. Please keep referring the website for latest information.

4. Submission of Copyright Form

  1. IEEE policy requires that every submitted paper must be accompanied by a signed IEEE Copyright Form before publication can occur.
  2. The electronic copyright submission form will walk you through the steps to complete and submit the IEEE copyright form for your paper. Only one form is required per paper.
  3. After submission of the final camera-ready paper on CMT (Please ensure the paper must have passed PDF Check in IEEE PDF eXpress), click on the 'Submit IEEE copyright Form' link to enter the IEEE Copyright Form Submission CMT page. Then, click on the IEEE copyright website link. Now, the author will be redirected to the IEEE eCF site to submit the copyright form.
  4. After filling out the IEEE copyright form and copyright agreements, there is an option to download the completed IEEE Copyright Form (The corresponding author would have received a copy of the copyright form through email)
  5. Please upload it to the same page in CMT (or use the 'drop files here' menu). The corresponding author may submit the copyright form for all the authors of the paper
  6. For more information on the electronic copyright process, please check https://www.ieee.org/content/dam/ieee-org/ieee/web/org/pubs/ecf_faq.pdf
  7. Please ensure that the title of your paper is correct and that the author names and the affiliation details are correctly recorded in the CMT system before completing the IEEE electronic copyright form. You will not be able to change/edit the form once it is submitted.

5. Conference Registration

  1. A conference registration is mandatory to consider the paper for inclusion in the conference program and the proceedings. At least, one author of the paper must register for the conference.
  2. All participants (authors/co-authors/non-authors) need to individually register for the conference.
  3. A full registration will cover 01 paper only. If more authors from the same accepted paper want to attend the conference, then they need to register separately as per registration. Please check the registration guidelines on the conference website for registration and payment information.
  4. Registered author will be allowed for oral presentation in-person. The registered and presented papers will only be submitted for publication in conference proceedings on IEEE Xplore.
