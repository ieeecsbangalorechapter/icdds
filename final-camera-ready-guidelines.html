Camera-Ready Submission

After acceptance notification, authors of accepted papers must prepare and submit the final camera-ready version following the guidelines below.

Step 1: Revise Your Manuscript

  1. Address all reviewer comments carefully.
  2. Ensure your paper does not exceed 6 pages (including references). Up to 2 additional pages may be purchased.
  3. Verify double-blind review requirements are lifted for camera-ready — include author names and affiliations.
  4. Use the IEEE two-column conference paper template.

Step 2: IEEE PDF eXpress

  1. Create an account at IEEE PDF eXpress (Conference ID will be provided).
  2. Upload your manuscript and generate an IEEE Xplore-compatible PDF.
  3. Download and keep the approved PDF from PDF eXpress.

Step 3: IEEE Copyright Form

  1. Complete the IEEE Electronic Copyright Form (eCF) via the link provided after acceptance.
  2. Only the corresponding author needs to sign the copyright form.
  3. Make sure copyright transfer is done before uploading the final paper.

Step 4: Submit via CMT

  1. Log in to Microsoft CMT.
  2. Navigate to your accepted submission.
  3. Upload the IEEE PDF eXpress approved PDF.
  4. Fill in all required metadata (title, abstract, authors, keywords).
  5. Submit before the camera-ready deadline.

Step 5: Registration

  1. At least one author must register for the conference.
  2. Complete registration before the camera-ready deadline.
  3. See the Registration page for fee details.
