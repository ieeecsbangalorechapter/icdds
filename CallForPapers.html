ICDDS 2026 Invites Original Research

ICDDS 2026 welcomes high-quality original contributions from researchers, practitioners, and industry experts. We seek papers that combine technical rigor with practical impact in data-driven systems, AI, and decision support.

Foundational Research Systems Research Applications

Submission Tracks

Track I – Foundational Research: New theory, learning, optimization, reasoning, and models.
Track II – Systems Research: Infrastructure, algorithms, data platforms, and scalable systems.
Track III – Applications: Real-world deployments, case studies, and cross-domain solutions.

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must be original, unpublished, and relevant to the conference scope.
  • Use the IEEE conference template and submit in PDF format.
  • Manuscripts must not exceed 6 pages in IEEE two-column format, including references.
  • ICDDS 2026 uses double-blind review — remove all author identities.
  • All submissions are subject to IEEE plagiarism and AI-generated text policies.
