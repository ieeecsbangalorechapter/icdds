Conference Venue

Indian Institute of Information Technology, Dharwad

Ittigatti Road, Near Sattur Colony, Dharwad - 580009

IIIT Dharwad, established in 2015 under a Public-Private-Partnership (PPP) between the Ministry of Education, Government of Karnataka, and Keonics, is an Institute of National Importance aimed at bridging the skill gap in high-end IT sectors. It offers BTech programs in Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Data Science and AI, focusing on solving socially relevant problems in India. The institute’s semi-modern theme is reflected in its campus design and logo, symbolizing its vision to address emerging challenges in IT, robotics, data sciences, and cyber-physical systems. Strategically located in Hubballi-Dharwad, it is a growing educational hub. Also with proximity to Bengaluru, IIIT Dharwad is poised to make a significant impact on the Indian IT industry, academia, and the local community.
