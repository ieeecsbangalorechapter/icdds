Poster Session

The Poster Session offers a vibrant forum for researchers to showcase preliminary or ongoing work in the broad areas of Data and Decision Systems. Presenters will benefit from engaging conversations and constructive feedback from the reviewing committee and conference attendees. Accepted and presented posters will be submitted in IEEE Explore conference proceedings.

Submission Guidelines

Authors are encouraged to highlight the novelty and potential impact of their work. All poster submissions will undergo a double-blind peer review, so please ensure that no author names, affiliations, or identifying information are included in the manuscript. Submissions must be between 3 and 4 pages, including all technical content, figures, tables, and references. They must follow the IEEE double-column format (10-point font), consistent with the main conference formatting guidelines. All submissions should be in PDF format.

Poster Format for Printing

You will need to bring a printed copy of your accepted poster to the poster session. Please prepare your posters in dimensions: A0 (841 x 1189 mm or 33.11 x 46.81 inches) in portrait orientation. We will arrange for the necessary support system, including poster stands, clips, etc. Posters should be visually engaging, focusing on figures, diagrams, key results, and high-level takeaways rather than large blocks of text. Think of your poster as a conversation starter—a tool that sparks interest and encourages feedback.
