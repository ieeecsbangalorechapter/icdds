About ICDDS 2026

Rapid advances and growth in the connected world across different application domains are fast pacing the data volumes generated from a range of devices. ICDDS 2026 aims to look broadly at data-based innovation and technologies in interdisciplinary areas.

Track I – Foundational Research

Focused on theory, novel findings, and current practices in data and decision science.

Learn More

Track II – Systems Research

Addressing system aspects of data and decision science including IoT and Cyber Physical Systems.

Learn More

Track III – Applications

Real-world problems and their solutions using data and decision science.

Learn More

Accepted papers will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore subject to meeting IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements.

Established in 1969, CHRIST (Deemed to be University) stands as one of India's premier institutions, globally recognized for academic excellence and holistic development. The Yeshwanthpur campus features state-of-the-art infrastructure tailored for high-level academic conferences. Strategically located in North Bengaluru with exceptional transport connectivity, the campus provides an inspiring, world-class environment.
